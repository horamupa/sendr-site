What does the Reach app do?

Reach is a mass text app for iPhone and iPad that sends each message individually, so recipients don't see each other, much like BCC in email. Its store page lists distribution groups, name templates, CSV import and export, iCloud backup, Google Voice and sending through SMS, iMessage, WhatsApp or email.

Reach also lists two features none of the seven alternatives mention: automatic translation of a text into each recipient's language, and a CC recipient who gets a copy of every individual message. On a Mac with Apple silicon you can set up groups and messages, but sending happens on iPhone or iPad.

The free version sends to up to 8 contacts and 2 groups. Reach Unlimited is a monthly subscription. On the US App Store Reach has a 4.8 rating from 7,710 ratings.

Why look for a Reach alternative?

People usually look for a Reach alternative for one of three reasons: their list has outgrown the free limit of 8 contacts, they don't want a monthly subscription, or they want to see each person's text before it goes out. Every app here is free to download, and Sendr's Send One by One is free.

If you rely on translated texts or a CC recipient, stay with Reach: none of these seven list those features on their store pages.

Questions

What is the best alternative to the Reach app?

It depends on what you need. Hit Em Up can schedule messages, Group Text - Mass SMS Message comes in 32 languages, and Sendr lets you check and edit each person's text before it goes out. All of them send each person a separate message from your own number.

Is there a free alternative to Reach?

Yes. Send One by One in Sendr is free: it opens a message for each person and you tap Send. Group Text | Mass Message sends to up to five contacts at once for free.

Is the Reach app free?

Reach is free to download. The free version sends to up to 8 contacts and 2 groups. Unlimited texting, contacts and groups need the Reach Unlimited monthly subscription.

Which Reach alternative works with WhatsApp?

Hit Em Up and BCC Text both list WhatsApp on their store pages. Sendr sends WhatsApp messages to contacts set to the WhatsApp channel, text only, without photos.
