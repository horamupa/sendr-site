Can you BCC a text on iPhone? How to send a BCC text

No, you can't BCC a text on iPhone: Messages has no BCC field, and everyone you add to one message joins the same conversation. To get the result of a BCC text, send each person a separate message by hand, with a Shortcuts loop or with a mass text app. Then each person sees only your text.

What does BCC mean in a text?

BCC stands for blind carbon copy, a field from email. People in BCC get the message but can't see who else got it. Text messages have no such field. When people ask for a BCC text, they mean one text sent to many people where nobody sees the list and replies come back only to you.

Why is there no BCC in iPhone Messages?

Messages treats several recipients as a group. Apple says that in a group iMessage or group MMS everyone sees all the replies. In a group SMS, replies go out as individual texts and photos aren't supported, but Apple doesn't say the other numbers are hidden. So the only sure way to hide recipients is a separate message to each person.

For three or four people, copy your text, open a new message to one person, paste and send, then repeat. For more people, use one of the methods below.

How to send a BCC text with Shortcuts

Shortcuts comes with every iPhone and can send one message per person, which works like a BCC text. The loop is what keeps the messages separate.

  1. Open Shortcuts and tap the plus button to create a shortcut.
  2. Add the Contacts action and choose your recipients.
  3. Add Repeat with Each.
  4. Inside the loop, add Send Message. Type your text, set the recipient to Repeat Item and turn off Show When Run.
  5. Run the shortcut and allow it to send messages.

You get no report of who received the text, and the list lives inside the shortcut, so you edit it there.

How to send a BCC text with an app

A mass text app sends one text to many people as separate messages from your own number, so it gives you a BCC text without the setup. BCC Text says it sends one-to-one texts through Messages, Google Voice or WhatsApp. Hit Em Up and Sendr also send each person a separate message.

Questions

Can recipients see each other in a BCC text?

Not when each person gets a separate message. Each text arrives in a one-on-one thread with you, so there is no list of other recipients to see.

Where do replies to a BCC text go?

Each reply comes back to your one-on-one thread with that person. Other recipients never see it.

Does turning off iMessage make a group text BCC?

Not reliably. Apple only says that replies in a group SMS come back as individual texts. It doesn't promise that recipients can't see who else got the message.

Can a BCC text include photos?

Yes, if you send separate messages. A group SMS can't carry photos or videos, but a one-on-one iMessage or MMS can. Sendr adds up to 3 photos or videos to iMessage and SMS texts.
