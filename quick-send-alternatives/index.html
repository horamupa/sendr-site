What does the Quick Send app do?

Quick Send is a mass text app for iPhone that sends personalized texts to several contacts one by one from your own number, with no group chat and no reply-all. Fields for first name, last name and time fill in each message, and you can sort address book contacts into groups for quick access.

Quick Send Pro is a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription. The store page doesn't say what the free version includes. The app needs iOS 17.6 or later and its interface is in English only. On the US App Store it has a 4.7 rating from 766 ratings, and it was last updated on November 13, 2026.

Why look for a Quick Send alternative?

People usually look for a Quick Send alternative when they need something its store page doesn't mention: an interface in another language, WhatsApp, photos in the message or importing contacts from a CSV file. Others just want to try a free option first. Every app here is free to download, and Sendr's Send One by One is free.

If the time field is what you use most, stay with Quick Send: none of the seven list a time field on their store pages.

How we picked these apps

Every app here is on the US App Store, was updated in the last 18 months and says on its store page that each person gets a separate message sent from your own number. That rules out group chat apps like GroupMe, where everyone shares one thread. Ratings and prices were checked on the US App Store on November 22, 2026.

Questions

What is the best alternative to Quick Send?

It depends on what you need. Reach has the most ratings and can translate texts, Hit Em Up can schedule messages, Group Text - Mass SMS Message comes in 32 languages, and Sendr lets you check and edit each person's text before it goes out. All of them send each person a separate message from your own number.

Is there a free alternative to Quick Send?

Yes. Send One by One in Sendr is free: it opens a message for each person and you tap Send. Reach's free version sends to up to 8 contacts, and Group Text | Mass Message sends to up to five contacts at once for free.

Is the Quick Send app free?

Quick Send is free to download. Quick Send Pro is a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, and the store page doesn't list what works without it.

Which Quick Send alternative works with WhatsApp?

Reach, Hit Em Up and BCC Text list WhatsApp on their store pages. Sendr sends WhatsApp messages to contacts set to the WhatsApp channel, text only, without photos.
