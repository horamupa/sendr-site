Hit Em Up alternatives: 6 mass text apps for iPhone in 2026

The best Hit Em Up alternatives for iPhone in 2026 are Sendr, Reach, Group Text, SendHype, Quick Send and BCC Text. Like the Hit Em Up app, each one sends a single text to many people as separate messages from your own number. Unlike Hit Em Up, which needs a membership after a 7-day trial, all six are free to download.

We make Sendr, so it comes first. The other five apps are made by other developers, and nobody paid for a place here. We picked apps whose App Store page says each person gets a separate message.

What does the Hit Em Up app do?

Hit Em Up is a mass text app for iPhone that sends one message to a list of contacts, personalized for each person with inserts like ::firstname:: and delivered privately. Its App Store page lists SmartWrite AI for drafting, scheduled messages, templates, CSV import and sending over SMS, iMessage, WhatsApp or email.

The app is free to download and try for 7 days. After that it needs a Standard or Basic membership, billed monthly or yearly. The interface is in English only. On the US App Store it has a 4.6 rating from 4,924 ratings.

Why look for a Hit Em Up alternative?

Most people look for a Hit Em Up alternative for one of three reasons: they want to keep sending without a membership, they need the app in another language, or they want to see each person's text before it goes out. Every app on this list is free to download, and several have a free version.

Hit Em Up still has one thing none of these six list on their store pages: scheduled messages. If you rely on it, stay with Hit Em Up.

Questions

What is the best alternative to Hit Em Up?

It depends on what you need. Reach has the most ratings and sends over the most channels, Group Text comes in 15 languages, and Sendr lets you check and edit each person's text before it goes out. All of them send each person a separate message from your own number.

Is there a free alternative to Hit Em Up?

Yes. Send One by One in Sendr is free: it opens a message for each person and you tap Send. Reach's free version sends to up to 8 contacts, and Group Text's free version sends to up to five contacts at once.

Is Hit Em Up free?

Hit Em Up is free to download and try for 7 days with no credit card. After the trial it needs a Standard or Basic membership, paid monthly or yearly. Prices are shown inside the app.

Which Hit Em Up alternative can schedule texts?

None of the six apps here lists scheduled sending on its App Store page. Hit Em Up does. If scheduling matters most, Hit Em Up remains the better fit; if you want a free version or an app in your language, pick one of the alternatives.
