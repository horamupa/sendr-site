How to turn off group messaging on iPhone and stop group texts

To turn off group messaging on iPhone, open Settings, tap Apps, then Messages, and turn off MMS Messaging and RCS Messaging. That covers green group texts you send. No single switch blocks every group: a group iMessage has to be left or muted, and an unwanted MMS or RCS group can be deleted and blocked.

How do you turn off group messaging in Settings?

Turn off MMS Messaging and RCS Messaging in the Messages settings. Your iPhone then can't send a group MMS or RCS, so a text to several people who aren't all on iMessage goes as SMS. Apple says replies to a group SMS come back as individual texts.

  1. Open Settings, tap Apps, then tap Messages.
  2. Turn off MMS Messaging.
  3. Tap RCS Messaging and turn it off.
  4. If you see Group Messaging, turn that off too. Many iPhones don't show it.

There is a cost. Without MMS and RCS, photos and videos won't go to people who aren't on iMessage. Group iMessages keep working, because iMessage has its own switch on the same screen.

How to leave a group text on iPhone

You can leave a group text only when it is a group iMessage with at least three other people, all on Apple devices. If one person is on Android, or the group is too small, Messages won't let you leave, and muting is the way to go.

  1. Open the group in Messages.
  2. Tap the group icons at the top.
  3. Scroll down, tap Leave this Conversation and confirm.

The people left in the group can keep talking without you. Turning off iMessage isn't a substitute for leaving, because it changes how all your texts are sent.

How to stop getting group text notifications

Turn on Hide Alerts for the group. Messages still arrive, but your iPhone stays quiet about that one conversation and keeps notifying you about the rest. This works for green groups too, including those you can't leave.

  1. Open the group and tap the group icons at the top.
  2. Scroll down and turn on Hide Alerts.

The quicker way is to swipe left on the group in the list and tap the bell button.

For spam in an MMS or RCS group, tap the group name or the number of participants at the top and choose Delete and Block this Conversation. Apple added this in iOS 17.

How to text many people without starting a group

Send each person a separate message. Then there is no group to turn off: nobody sees the other recipients, and each reply comes back to your own thread with that person. For a few people, copy and paste. For a longer list, use a Shortcuts loop or a mass text app.

Questions

Where is Group Messaging in iPhone settings?

When it is there, it sits in Settings, Apps, Messages. Many iPhones don't show it on recent iOS versions. In that case, use MMS Messaging and RCS Messaging on the same screen.

How do I stop receiving group texts on my iPhone?

Apple's support pages list no switch that blocks all incoming groups. Leave a group iMessage, mute a group with Hide Alerts, or delete and block an MMS or RCS group.

Why can't I leave a group text?

Leaving needs three other people in the thread and everyone on iMessage. Groups with an Android phone, and groups of three, don't qualify. Turn on Hide Alerts instead.

Does turning off MMS stop group iMessages?

No. Group iMessages go through Apple, not your carrier, so the MMS switch doesn't affect them.
