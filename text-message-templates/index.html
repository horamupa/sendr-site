Text message templates: 25 texts to copy for reminders, follow-ups and updates

A good text message template says who you are, gives one piece of information and tells the reader what to do next, all in under 160 characters. Below are 25 templates for reminders, follow-ups, updates, invitations and review requests that you can copy, plus how to send one to a whole list as separate messages.

What makes a good text message template?

A good text message template has four parts: a greeting with the person's first name, your name or business, one clear piece of information and one next step. Keep it under 160 characters so it fits in a single SMS, and write it the way you would talk to that person at the counter.

Leave gaps for what changes. In the templates below, {first_name} and {company} are placeholders, and anything in square brackets, such as [date] or [time], is yours to fill in. Replace them by hand, or let an app fill in the name for each person.

One text should do one job. A reminder that also asks for a review and announces a sale gets none of the three done.

Appointment reminder text message templates

An appointment reminder text needs the date, the time and a way to confirm or reschedule. Send it the day before, during working hours, and once more a couple of hours ahead if no-shows cost you money. Put the date and time near the start, where they show in the notification preview.

  1. Hi {first_name}, this is [your name] from {company}. A reminder about your appointment on [date] at [time]. Reply YES to confirm.
  2. Hi {first_name}, see you tomorrow at [time]. If you need to reschedule, just reply to this text. [Your name]
  3. {first_name}, your appointment at {company} is today at [time]. Our address: [address]. See you soon!
  4. Hi {first_name}, it's been [six months] since your last visit to {company}. Want me to book you in? Reply with a day that suits you.
  5. Hi {first_name}, we missed you today. Would you like to rebook? Reply with a time that works. [Your name]

Follow-up text message templates

A follow-up text works best within a day of the meeting, visit or quote, while the person still remembers the details. Mention what you talked about, ask one question and make it easy to answer with a single word. If there is no reply, follow up once more and then stop.

  1. Hi {first_name}, thanks for coming in today. Any questions about [topic]? Just reply here. [Your name]
  2. Hi {first_name}, I sent the quote for [project] on [day]. Does it look right, or should I change anything? [Your name]
  3. {first_name}, checking in after [service]. Is everything working the way it should? [Your name], {company}
  4. Hi {first_name}, great to meet you at [event]. Here's my number, as promised. [Your name], {company}
  5. Hi {first_name}, I haven't heard back, so I'll leave it here. If [project] comes up again, text me any time. [Your name]

Announcement and update text templates

An update text gives the news in the first sentence: what changed, from when, and what the person should do, if anything. Use it for new opening hours, closures, schedule changes and orders that are ready. People act on these texts, so check the dates twice before you send.

  1. Hi {first_name}, {company} is closed on [date]. We're back on [date] at [time]. Thanks for understanding!
  2. Hi {first_name}, from [date] our new hours are [hours]. Same place, same team. {company}
  3. {first_name}, your order is ready for pickup at {company}. We're open until [time] today.
  4. Hi {first_name}, practice on [day] moves to [time] at [place]. Please reply so I know you saw this. [Your name]
  5. Hi {first_name}, we've moved! Our new address is [address], starting [date]. {company}

Invitation and event text templates

An invitation by text needs five facts: what, when, where, who is invited and how to answer. Send it two to three weeks ahead for a party and a few days ahead for a meeting, then send a short reminder the day before. Ask for a reply by a date so you can plan.

  1. Hi {first_name}, you're invited to [event] on [date] at [time], [place]. Can you make it? Reply YES or NO by [date].
  2. {first_name}, parents' meeting on [date] at [time] in [room]. It takes about [40 minutes]. Please reply if you can't come. [Your name]
  3. Hi {first_name}, {company} is hosting an open house on [date], [time] to [time]. Bring a friend. We'd love to see you!
  4. Hi {first_name}, a reminder: [event] is tomorrow at [time]. Parking is [where]. See you there! [Your name]
  5. {first_name}, thank you for coming to [event]. Photos are here: [link]. [Your name]

Review, referral and payment reminder templates

Ask for a review or a referral right after a good result, and ask once. A payment reminder should state the amount, the due date and how to pay, in a neutral tone. In all three cases the text goes to someone you have already worked with, never to a cold list.

  1. Hi {first_name}, thank you for choosing {company}. If you have a minute, a short review would help us a lot: [link]
  2. {first_name}, I'm glad [result] worked out. If a friend needs [service], feel free to pass on my number. [Your name]
  3. Hi {first_name}, a friendly reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] is due on [date]. You can pay here: [link]. Thank you! {company}
  4. Hi {first_name}, invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [date]. Could you let me know when to expect payment? [Your name]
  5. Hi {first_name}, payment received, thank you! [Your name], {company}

Questions

How long should a text message template be?

Under 160 characters if it may go out as SMS. An emoji or a non-Latin character lowers the limit to 70, and a longer text is split into parts.

How do I save a text message template on iPhone?

Messages has no template feature. You can keep your texts in Notes and paste them, set up a shortcut phrase in Settings, General, Keyboard, Text Replacement, or use an app that stores templates.

Do I need permission to send business texts?

For promotional texts, yes in most countries: the person has to agree first and be able to opt out. Rules for reminders and service updates are usually lighter, but they differ by country, so check them where your clients live.

How do I personalize a template for a long list?

Put a placeholder such as {first_name} where the name goes and send each person a separate message. Add one line by hand for the clients who matter most.
