Thank you messages to clients: 20 text templates for every occasion

A good thank you message to a client is two or three sentences long, uses their first name, names one specific thing you're grateful for and asks for nothing. Below are 20 texts for clients and customers that you can copy, grouped by occasion, plus how to send them one by one.

What should a thank you message to a client say?

A thank you message to a client should say who you are, what you're thanking them for and nothing else. One real detail beats a polished sentence: the kitchen you finished, the color they picked, the question they asked. Leave out links and offers, because a thank you that sells reads like an ad.

Sign with your own name, since a client may not have your number saved. Keep it to something you'd say out loud at the counter.

In the templates, {first_name} and {company} are placeholders. Replace them by hand, or let an app fill them in for each client.

Thank you messages after a first purchase or visit

Send a thank you message to a new customer on the day they buy, while the visit is still fresh. Name what they bought or booked and tell them how to reach you if something goes wrong. This is the text that turns a one-time buyer into someone who has your number.

  1. Hi {first_name}, thank you for stopping by {company} today. If anything isn't right, just text me back. [Your name]
  2. Thank you for your first order, {first_name}! It means a lot to a small business like ours. {company}
  3. {first_name}, it was great to meet you today. Thanks for trusting me with this. I'm here if you have questions. [Your name]
  4. Hi {first_name}, thank you for your purchase. I hope you love it, and I'd like to hear how it goes. [Your name]

Thank you messages after finishing a project

After a project, thank the client within two days of handing it over, and mention one thing that went well. It lands better before the invoice than after it. Clients remember the last message they got from you, so this one shouldn't be about money or next steps.

  1. Hi {first_name}, the project is done, and I wanted to say thank you for trusting me with it. It was a pleasure. [Your name]
  2. {first_name}, thank you for your quick answers on this job. They made a real difference to the result. [Your name]
  3. Thank you, {first_name}. I really enjoyed working on this with you, and I'm proud of how it turned out. [Your name]
  4. Hi {first_name}, thank you for choosing {company} for this project. If anything comes up, you know where to find us.

Thank you messages for loyal clients

A loyal client hears from you less than a new one, which is exactly why a short thank you works. Send it on no particular occasion, or on the anniversary of their first order, and say how long you've worked together. Don't attach a discount, or the thanks starts to look like a sales tactic.

  1. Hi {first_name}, I realized it's been three years since your first visit. Thank you for sticking with us. [Your name]
  2. {first_name}, no reason for this text, I just wanted to say thanks for being a client of {company}.
  3. Thank you for your continued support, {first_name}. Clients like you are why I enjoy this work. [Your name]
  4. Hi {first_name}, you've been with me from the start, and I haven't forgotten it. Thank you. [Your name]

Thank you messages for a referral or a review

Thank a client for a referral or a review within a day, and say who they sent or what they wrote. People who recommend you are doing you a favor in public, so a quick and specific reply tells them it mattered. Don't ask for anything else in the same text.

  1. Hi {first_name}, thank you for sending [friend's name] my way. That kind of trust means a lot. [Your name]
  2. {first_name}, I just read your review. Thank you! It really helps a small business like ours. [Your name]
  3. Thank you for recommending {company}, {first_name}. Most of our new clients come from people like you.
  4. Hi {first_name}, [friend's name] told me you recommended me. Thank you, I owe you one. [Your name]

End of year thank you messages to clients

An end of year thank you message looks back and leaves the holiday greeting out, so it works for every client regardless of what they celebrate. Send it between December 15 and 30, and mention one thing you did together that year. It suits clients you haven't spoken to in months, too.

  1. Hi {first_name}, thank you for working with me this year. I hope 2027 is a good one for you. [Your name]
  2. {first_name}, before the year ends, I wanted to say thank you for your business. It meant a lot to {company}.
  3. Looking back at this year, working with you was a highlight, {first_name}. Thank you. [Your name]
  4. Thank you for trusting us in 2026, {first_name}. All of us at {company} appreciate it.

Questions

How do you say thank you for your business in a text?

Keep it plain: "Hi {first_name}, thank you for your business. It means a lot to us." Then add one detail about what they bought or what you did together, so it doesn't read like a form letter.

How do I reply when a client says thank you?

Say you were glad to help and leave the door open: "You're welcome, {first_name}. It was a pleasure. Text me anytime." One line is enough.

Is a text or a handwritten note better?

A handwritten note takes more effort, so it counts for more with your biggest clients. A text is better when speed matters, like the same day as a purchase, and for clients you already text with.

Can I add a discount to a thank you message?

Send it separately. A thank you with a code reads like an ad, and in many countries promotional texts need the client's prior consent. Check the rules where your clients live.
