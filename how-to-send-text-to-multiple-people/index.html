How to send a text to multiple people without a group chat

To send a text to multiple people without a group chat, the message has to go to each person separately. On Android, turn on the mass text option in Google Messages. On iPhone, Messages starts a group whenever you add several people, so use group SMS with iMessage off, a Shortcuts loop or a mass text app.

Why does texting several people start a group chat?

Both iPhone and Android treat a message with several recipients as a group conversation by default. Everyone sees the full list of names, and every reply goes to the whole group. To keep people apart, the phone has to send a separate copy to each person, and replies then come back to your one-on-one threads.

How to text multiple people on Android without a group

Google Messages has a setting that sends one text to many people as separate SMS messages. Google calls it mass text: each person gets the text on their own and replies only to you. Samsung is moving Galaxy phones to Google Messages, so the same steps work on most Galaxy phones.

  1. Open Google Messages.
  2. Tap your profile photo or icon, then tap Messages settings.
  3. Tap Advanced, then Group messaging.
  4. Choose mass text instead of group MMS.
  5. Start a new conversation, add your recipients and send.

The other option, group MMS, puts everyone in one conversation where all replies are shared. Switch back to it later if you want normal group chats again.

How to text multiple people on iPhone without a group

On iPhone, Messages has no mass text switch. With iMessage on, adding several people to one message always creates a group. You can turn off iMessage and send a group SMS, build a Shortcut that sends one message per contact, or use a mass text app.

Turning off iMessage changes how all your texts go out until you turn it back on, and Apple notes that group SMS can't carry photos or videos. A Shortcuts loop keeps the list inside the shortcut and gives you no report of who got the text. Our iPhone guide walks through both methods step by step.

How to send each person a personal text

Phone settings send the exact same text to everyone. If you want each message to start with the person's first name, you need a mass text app that fills in names for you, and on iPhone those apps still send through Messages from your own number.

In Sendr, write your text with {first_name} where the name goes, pick contacts or a saved group, and check every message on the Review & Send screen. You can edit any single message there. Questions

Can recipients see each other when I text multiple people?

In a group chat, yes. When each person gets a separate message, they see only your number and their own copy of the text.

How do I send a text to multiple contacts on Samsung?

Most current Galaxy phones use Google Messages. Turn on mass text under Messages settings, Advanced, Group messaging, then add your contacts to one new message.

Do replies go to everyone?

Not with mass text or separate messages. Each reply comes back only to you, in your one-on-one conversation with that person.

Can I send photos to multiple people without a group?

On iPhone, group SMS with iMessage off can't carry photos. Sendr sends up to 3 photos or videos with each separate iMessage or SMS.
