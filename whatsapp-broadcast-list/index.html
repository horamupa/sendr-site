What is a WhatsApp broadcast list?

A broadcast list is a saved set of contacts in WhatsApp that you can message again and again without picking them each time. WhatsApp delivers the message to each person as an individual message in their Chats tab. A reply appears as a normal message to you and isn't sent to the other contacts.

You can create as many lists as you like, with up to 256 contacts in each. Broadcast lists work on iPhone and Android only. WhatsApp says they aren't supported on Windows, Mac or Web.

How to create a broadcast list on iPhone

On iPhone, broadcast lists live in WhatsApp settings. Creating a list takes under a minute, and the list stays saved for the next message.

  1. Open WhatsApp and tap Settings or your profile picture.
  2. Tap Broadcast messages, then New List.
  3. Search for or select the contacts you want to add.
  4. Tap Create.
  5. Type your message in the list and send it.

To rename the list or change who is in it, go back to Broadcast messages and tap the i icon next to the list. To delete a list, swipe left on it and tap Delete.

How to create a broadcast list on Android

On Android, you create a broadcast list from the Chats tab. The steps come from the WhatsApp Help Center.

  1. Open WhatsApp and tap Chats.
  2. Tap the menu icon, then Broadcast lists.
  3. Tap the button for a new list.
  4. Search for or select the contacts you want to add.
  5. Tap the confirm button, then type your message and send it.

The list then appears in your Chats tab. Open it and choose Broadcast list info to rename it, edit recipients or delete it.

Why do some contacts not get a broadcast message?

Only people who have saved your number in their phone's contacts receive a broadcast message. WhatsApp shows no error for the others, the message just never arrives. If someone isn't getting your broadcasts, WhatsApp's advice is to ask that person to add you to their contacts.

There is also a monthly limit in some countries. Where it applies, you can send up to 35 free broadcast messages per month, and WhatsApp notifies you when you are close to it. The limit is rolling out gradually, so you may not see it yet. You can check it in WhatsApp settings.

Questions

Do people in a broadcast list know they are on it?

WhatsApp delivers a broadcast as an individual message from you in the recipient's Chats tab. Their reply comes back to you as a normal message and isn't sent to the other contacts in the list.

How many contacts can a WhatsApp broadcast list have?

Up to 256 contacts in each list. You can create as many lists as you like.

Is there a limit on WhatsApp broadcast messages?

In some countries, yes. WhatsApp is rolling out a limit of 35 free broadcast messages per month. If you don't see a limit in your settings, it doesn't apply to you yet.

Can I use broadcast lists on WhatsApp Web?

No. WhatsApp says broadcast lists aren't supported on Windows, Mac or Web. Create and use them on your phone.

What is the difference between a broadcast list and a group?

A broadcast list is one-to-many: each person gets a private message and replies only to you. In a group chat, everyone sees all the messages and all the members.
