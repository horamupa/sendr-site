What does the BCC Text app do?

BCC Text is a mass text app for iPhone that sends individual messages to many people at once from your own number, through Messages, Google Voice or WhatsApp, chosen per contact. Each person gets a private text and no group chat is created. With Shortcuts it sends to a whole group with one tap.

Its store page also lists first-name tokens, unlimited groups, reusable templates, CSV import and photos when you send through Messages. The description doesn't mention a subscription or prices. The app needs iOS 16 or later and its interface is in English only. On the US App Store it has a 4.3 rating from 159 ratings, and it was last updated on October 31, 2026.

Why look for a BCC Text alternative?

People usually look for a BCC Text alternative when they need something its store page doesn't mention: an interface in another language, a preview of every person's text before sending, scheduled messages, video attachments or fields beyond the first name, like last name and company. Others want an app with more ratings to judge by: BCC Text has 159 on the US App Store, while Reach has over 7,700. Every app here is free to download.

If you send through Google Voice, stay with BCC Text or look at Reach: they are the only two here that list Google Voice on their store pages.
