The best mass text apps for iPhone in 2026 are Sendr, Reach, Group Text, Hit Em Up, SendHype, Quick Send and BCC Text. Each one sends a single text to many people as separate messages from your own number, so nobody ends up in a group chat. They differ in price, name personalization and extra channels.

How we picked these apps

Every app here is on the US App Store, was updated in the last 18 months and says on its store page that each person gets a separate message sent from your own number. That rules out group chat apps like GroupMe, where everyone shares one thread. Ratings and prices were checked on the US App Store on November 7, 2026.

We left out apps whose store page doesn't say messages go out one by one, and SMS marketing platforms built around a business account.

Mass text app or SMS marketing platform?

A mass text app sends from your own iPhone through Messages, so replies land in your usual threads and each text costs the same as one you type by hand. An SMS marketing platform such as SimpleTexting is a business service: its App Store page lists campaigns to subscriber lists, text-to-join keywords, analytics and a shared team inbox.

Pick a platform when people sign up to your list and you need to track subscribers and unsubscribes. Pick an app from this list when you text people who already have your number: clients, a team, a church group or parents from a class.

Questions

What is the best mass text app for iPhone?

It depends on what you send. Reach has the most ratings in this list, Hit Em Up adds AI drafting and scheduling, and Sendr lets you check and edit each person's text before it goes out. All seven send each person a separate message.

Is there a free mass texting app for iPhone?

Yes. Send One by One in Sendr is free: it opens a message for each person and you tap Send. Group Text's free version sends to up to five contacts at once, and Hit Em Up has a 7-day free trial.

Do these apps send texts from my own number?

Yes. They send through the Messages app on your iPhone, so iMessage and SMS come from your number and replies land in your usual threads. Reach and BCC Text can also send through Google Voice.

Can a mass text app send without tapping Send for each person?

Only with a Shortcut. Send Automatically in Sendr Pro uses a one-time Shortcut setup and runs while your iPhone stays unlocked. BCC Text also lists sending a whole group through Shortcuts.
