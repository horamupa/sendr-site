How to send bulk messages on WhatsApp without breaking the rules

To send bulk messages on WhatsApp, use a broadcast list: it sends one message to up to 256 contacts as separate chats. For larger or business sends, WhatsApp offers the paid Business Platform. Unofficial bulk sender tools are risky, because WhatsApp's terms ban bulk and automated messaging and accounts that spam get banned.

Is bulk messaging allowed on WhatsApp?

Not in the sense most bulk sender tools promise. WhatsApp's Terms of Service list bulk messaging, auto-messaging and auto-dialing as uses that aren't allowed, and WhatsApp says it bans accounts involved in spam. A banned number shows a message that it can't use WhatsApp, and chat history becomes inaccessible.

What WhatsApp does allow is sending to many people through its own features: broadcast lists, forwarding, groups and, for companies, the Business Platform. Browser extensions and apps that log into your account and blast messages are not one of them.

How to send a bulk message with a broadcast list

A broadcast list is WhatsApp's own way to message many people at once. Each person gets your message as a normal one-to-one chat and replies only to you. A list holds up to 256 contacts, and you can make as many lists as you like.

  1. On iPhone, open WhatsApp, tap Settings, then Broadcast messages.
  2. Tap New List and select contacts.
  3. Tap Create, type your message and send it.

Two limits matter. Only people who saved your number in their contacts receive the message. And in some countries WhatsApp is rolling out a cap of 35 free broadcast messages per month.

How to forward one message to several chats

Forwarding is the quickest way to send something you already wrote. WhatsApp lets you forward a message to up to five chats at a time. If a message has been forwarded through many chats, it is marked as forwarded many times and can go to only one chat at a time.

  1. Press and hold the message.
  2. Tap Forward.
  3. Select up to five chats and tap Send.

Forwarding works for a handful of people. For dozens, a broadcast list saves you from repeating it.

How businesses send bulk WhatsApp messages

Companies that need to reach thousands of customers use the WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta. Messages go out through an API, and Meta charges for each delivered message. The price depends on the message category, such as marketing or utility, and on the recipient's country.

The rules are strict. You can only message people who opted in to hear from you, and a conversation that you start must use an approved template. This is the official route for marketing sends, but it needs setup and a budget, which makes it overkill for a coach, a club or a small team.

How to send a personal WhatsApp message to each person

Questions

How do I send a message to multiple contacts on WhatsApp?

Create a broadcast list with up to 256 contacts, or forward a message to up to five chats at a time. Both send separate chats, so recipients don't see each other.

Can WhatsApp ban you for bulk messaging?

Yes. WhatsApp's terms prohibit bulk and automated messaging, and WhatsApp bans accounts it believes are sending spam. Only WhatsApp can ban or unban an account.

How many messages can I send on WhatsApp at once?

A broadcast list reaches up to 256 contacts. In some countries there is a limit of 35 free broadcast messages per month. Forwarding is limited to five chats at a time.

Are WhatsApp bulk sender extensions safe?

WhatsApp doesn't support unofficial apps and bans accounts that spam. Sending through a third-party tool that automates your personal account puts the number at risk.
