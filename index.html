How it works

  1. Write the text once

    Add {first_name}, {company} or your own fields where the details should go.

  2. Pick the people

    Choose from your contacts, a saved group or a CSV file.

  3. Check every message

    Review & Send shows the exact text each person will get. Tap one to change it.

  4. Send

    Send One by One opens a message for each person and you tap Send. With Pro, Send Automatically goes through the list for you.

What's in the app

  • Personal details in every text

    Use {first_name}, {last_name}, {name}, {company} and fields you create.

  • Groups you can reuse

    Save clients, a team or event guests once and text them again in a few taps.

  • Templates and drafts

    Save the messages you send often, and keep a draft for later.

  • Photos and videos

    Attach up to 3 to a message that goes by iMessage or SMS.

  • WhatsApp

    Set WhatsApp as a contact's channel to send them the text there. WhatsApp gets text only.

  • A report after each send

    See who is on the Sent and Not Sent lists. Send to Remaining continues with anyone left.

Good to know before your first send

  • Send Automatically needs one setup: you add the Sendr shortcut to Apple's Shortcuts app.
  • Keep your iPhone unlocked during Send Automatically. If it locks, sending pauses and you can continue where it stopped.
  • Texts go out through the Messages app. iOS chooses iMessage or SMS, and SMS costs what your plan charges.
  • Sendr is for iPhone only. There is no Android version.
  • Text people who expect to hear from you. Carriers filter bulk messages, and no app can guarantee delivery.

Questions

How do I send a mass text on iPhone without a group chat?

Use an app that sends each person a separate message. In Sendr you write one text, pick your contacts and send. Every person gets an individual message from your number, so no group chat starts.

Can people see who else got my text?

No. Each person gets a separate message, so nobody sees the other recipients.

Do the messages come from my own number?

Yes. iMessage and SMS go through the Messages app on your iPhone. Sendr has no server in the middle, and replies come back to Messages.

Is Sendr free?

The download is free, and Send One by One works without Pro. Send Automatically and AI rewriting need Pro.

Does Sendr work with WhatsApp?

Yes, for text. Set WhatsApp as a contact's channel. Photos and videos go only by iMessage or SMS.

Does Sendr upload my contacts?

No. Contacts and messages stay on your iPhone, and you don't need an account. The app sends anonymous usage and crash data, described in the privacy policy.
