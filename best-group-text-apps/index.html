What is a group text app?

A group text app sends one message to a saved list of contacts. There are two kinds. A group chat puts everyone in one thread, and every reply goes to the whole group. The apps in this list send the same text to each person separately from your own number, so people don't see each other and replies come only to you.

SendHype and MassTexter offer both modes. The other six send separate texts only.

How we picked these apps

Every app here is on the US App Store, was updated in the last 18 months and says on its store page that each person gets a separate message sent from your own number. Ratings and prices were checked on the US App Store on November 24, 2026.

We left out apps whose store page doesn't say both things, chat apps where everyone shares one thread, and SMS marketing platforms built around a business account.

When is a group chat the better choice?

A group chat is the better choice when people need to talk to each other: a family planning a trip, a team picking a meeting time, friends splitting a bill. A group conversation in Messages or a chat app like GroupMe works for that, because everyone sees every reply.

Pick an app from this list when the answer matters only to you: appointment reminders, invitations, holiday greetings to clients or a schedule change for parents. Each person gets what looks like a personal text, and the others in the group stay hidden.

Questions

What is the best group text app for iPhone?

It depends on the group. Reach has the most ratings in this list, Group Text imports the groups you already keep in iCloud, and Sendr lets you check and edit each person's text before it goes out. All eight send each person a separate message from your own number.

Can I send a group text without starting a group chat?

Yes. Every app in this list sends the same text to each person as a separate message, so nobody sees the other recipients and replies come only to you. In Sendr, Reach, Group Text, Hit Em Up, Quick Send and BCC Text this is the only way of sending.

Is there a free group text app for iPhone?

Yes. Send One by One in Sendr is free: it opens a message for each person and you tap Send. Group Text's free version sends to up to five contacts at once, SendHype's free version covers small groups and a few messages, and Hit Em Up has a 7-day free trial.

Which group text apps can also send a group chat message?

SendHype and MassTexter. Both let you choose between individual texts and one group chat message. The other apps in this list send separate texts only.
